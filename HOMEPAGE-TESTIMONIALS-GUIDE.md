# Updating homepage testimonials

These steps become available after the updated Studio has been deployed and the initial five testimonials have been added.

1. Open https://successfulbob.sanity.studio/ and sign in.
2. Choose **Homepage Testimonials** in the left menu.
3. Open a person's entry to edit their **Name**, **Title and company**, **Quote**, or **Portrait**. Enter the quote without surrounding quotation marks; the website adds those.
4. To add someone, choose **Add item**, fill in their details, and upload their portrait. A square photo works best. Adjust the image hotspot if the face needs a different crop.
5. To change the order, drag entries using their handles. The first entry appears first on the homepage.
6. To remove someone, open the entry's menu and choose **Remove**. Removing every entry hides the entire testimonial section.
7. Click **Publish**. The website updates through the configured publishing webhook. If an update does not appear promptly, contact Pablo.

You do not need to edit code or change carousel settings. Visitors can choose a person, use the arrow buttons, or swipe horizontally on their phone. Quotes advance every seven seconds. A progress bar shows the time until the next quote. Hover over the section or focus a control to pause while reading, or use the Pause button. Choose Play and move the pointer away to resume.
